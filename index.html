FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is InstaStencil really free?

Yes! InstaStencil is completely free with no hidden costs. All processing happens in your browser — we don't even have a server to pay for.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, WEBP, and GIF. For best results, use high-contrast images with clear subjects. Maximum file size is 20MB.

Is my image uploaded to your servers?

No. All processing is done entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

What resolution will my stencil be?

The stencil output is the same resolution as your input image. For best print quality, use images of at least 1000×1000 pixels.

Can I use the stencils commercially?

Yes! You retain full rights to any stencil you create. Use them for personal projects, client work, or commercial products.

What's the difference between Threshold, Edge Detect, and Sketch modes?

Threshold creates pure black-and-white stencils by cutting at a brightness level. Edge Detect traces the edges and outlines of objects, great for line-art stencils. Sketch combines both to produce a pencil-sketch effect ideal for multi-layer stencils.
